## How this run was done, and where it is weaker than it looks

Every score below was produced by applying the rubric in `modes/_custom.md` to
the **live posting text**, re-fetched through the Indeed connector on
2026-08-26, against `cv.md`. It was done this way deliberately: scoring from the
`label.*_evidence` fields already stored in the case files would have tested
this run against notes written during the same exercise that produced the
labels, and agreed with itself.

**11 of 15 cases were re-fetched live. Four were not, and are marked ⚠ below.**

## Findings that matter more than the two criteria

### 1. The binding constraint is PM tenure, not domain

This is the single most actionable thing the run surfaced, and neither criterion
was looking for it.

`cv.md` shows **8.6 years total industry** (Jan 2018 →) but **~3.6 years in
PM-titled roles** (Jan 2023 →). The first five years are `Consultant – SCM
Systems`. Three roles that should have scored well on domain did not, purely on
that bar:

| Case | Asks for | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `jfrog-…` | 12+ years industry, **6+ years PM** | 3.0 not 4+ |
| `freshworks-…` | **9+ years dedicated SaaS PM**, 5+ B2B SaaS | 3.0 not 4+ |
| `trellix-…` | 7+ years PM **and 5+ years endpoint security** | compounded with a hard domain gate |

The README predicted `jfrog` and `freshworks` as ≥ 4 candidates on domain and
seniority signals. **Both came in at 3.0.** The prediction was made from the
posting; the score is made against the CV, and that is exactly the gap a
calibration run exists to expose.

**What to do about it is a CV question, not a rubric question.** The consulting
years at VOIS include requirements gathering, UAT, a 16-person team and a global
SAP WMS rollout — product work under a consultant title. If that is arguable,
the summary line should make the case explicitly rather than leaving a reader to
count title dates. Nothing here should be changed without the candidate's
sign-off: the dates are the dates.

### 2. One case label is wrong

`qad-senior-pm-pune` carries `"is_ai_role": false` and domain "ERP / supply
chain". The live posting opens: *"We are seeking a dynamic and results-driven
**AI Product Manager** to spearhead both internal and external AI-powered
product initiatives"*, for QAD's AI Platform — *"the domain intelligence layer
that sits between the ERP and autonomous AI agents"*.

It is an AI role with an ERP/SCM substrate. The label should be corrected, and
the case is more valuable relabelled than removed: it is the one posting whose
title says nothing about AI and whose body is entirely about it — the mirror
image of `google-…` (title says AI, body does not) and `pwc-…` (AI as a
keyword). **Not corrected here** — changing a case file mid-run would alter the
set this run was measured against. Raised for a separate change.

### 3. Three of fifteen cases can no longer be resolved

`pwc-…`, `barclays-…` and `google-…` could not be re-fetched. The cause is the
same defect `AGENTS.md` describes for JD captures: the case files store a
`to.indeed.com` shortlink, which does not map back to a job ID, so a case can be
found again only while the posting is still live and still surfaces on the same
search terms. Twenty percent of the set became unverifiable within one day.

Storing the connector's `job_id` alongside the URL would fix it. Worth doing
before the set is used again.

### 4. Two Block G signals worth recording

- **`onelab-ai-pm-pune`** gates applications on graduating from a named list of
  institutions. Legal in India, and a hard bar this candidate does not clear.
- **`unitedhealth-tpm-pune`** lists exactly one "Primary Responsibility", and it
  is not a product responsibility: *"Comply with the terms and conditions of the
  employment contract, company policies and procedures, and any and all
  directives (such as, but not limited to, transfer and/or re-assignment to
  different work locations, change in teams and/or work shifts) … The Company
  may adopt, vary or rescind these policies and directives in its absolute
  discretion."* Worth reading before applying.
