<!-- ===========================================================================
     Copy to interview-prep/story-bank.md and fill in. That path is gitignored
     (`interview-prep/*`) because it holds your real, unredacted achievements;
     this template is the committed shape.

     Parsed by match-star.mjs, which reads `### [Theme] Title` blocks and the
     bolded S/T/A/R labels below.

     Every field ships as the literal `_TODO_`. That is load-bearing, not
     laziness: match-star's field regex is `**Label:**\s*(.+)`, and `\s*` will
     happily cross a newline — so a field left genuinely blank does not get
     skipped, it swallows the NEXT line and reports "**R (Result):**" as its own
     action. An explicit placeholder keeps each block parsing predictably.

     The consequence to know about: until you fill these in, `node
     match-star.mjs` will match and return TODO stories. That is the intended
     nudge, and it is safe — `_TODO_` is visibly not a claim about you. What
     must never happen is a plausible-sounding invented achievement sitting
     here, because these stories feed interview answers and ATS form fields.
     Write only things you did.

     ── The one rule that matters ──────────────────────────────────────────────

     LEAD WITH THE OUTCOME, NOT THE STACK. A PM story is judged on adoption,
     revenue, cost saved, cycle time, and decisions influenced. It is not judged
     on which database you chose. Engineering-stack detail belongs in a story
     only where it is the evidence for a product decision — "we picked
     event-sourcing so partners could replay a day's orders, which is what
     unblocked the top-3 account" is a product sentence; "we used Kafka" is not.

     Every **R (Result):** line must carry a NUMBER and a BASELINE. "Improved
     onboarding" is not a result. "Partner onboarding 6 weeks → 5 days across 40
     partners" is. If you cannot name the baseline, the story is not ready.

     ── Provenance ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

     `node story-provenance-check.mjs` cross-checks quantified claims here
     against cv.md. A number that appears in a story but not in your CV is
     flagged: story-bank narrative is trusted for PHRASING, never as an
     independent source of fact. Keep the two consistent, and keep both true.
     =========================================================================== -->
